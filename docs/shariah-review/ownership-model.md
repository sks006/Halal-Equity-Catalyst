# Legal & Structural Ownership Model

## 1. Concept of Ownership (*Milkiyyah*) in Shariah

In Islamic law, valid ownership (*Milkiyyah Taammah*) confers:
1. **Right of Possession & Disposition (*Tasarruf*)**: The owner has the unimpeded right to hold, transfer, sell, or redeem the asset.
2. **Right to Usufruct & Growth (*Nama'*)**: Entitlement to legitimate dividends, profits, or capital appreciation generated by the underlying enterprise.
3. **Liability for Risk of Loss (*Dhaman*)**: The owner bears the genuine market risk of capital loss (*Al-Kharaj bi al-Dhaman* / *Al-Ghunm bi al-Ghurm*).

## 2. Technical Implementation on Solana

### 2.1 Direct Self-Custody
- Tokenized equity tokens are credited directly to the user's non-custodial Solana wallet (Associated Token Account).
- No intermediary or protocol retains discretionary withdrawal or rehypothecation power over user tokens.
- The user can transfer, hold, or sell the tokens autonomously without requiring centralized administrative approval.

### 2.2 Constructive Possession (*Qabd Hukmi*)
Under AAOIFI Shariah Standard No. 18 (*Possession*), possession can be either physical (*Haqiqi*) or constructive (*Hukmi*).
- Constructive possession occurs when the buyer obtains unimpeded control over the asset, enabling them to dispose of it freely, even if physical delivery of paper certificates is absent.
- On Solana, finality is achieved when a block is confirmed (~400 milliseconds). Upon confirmation:
  1. The token balance increases in the user's cryptographic account.
  2. The user has exclusive cryptographic signing authority over the tokens.
  3. This constitutes valid constructive possession (*Qabd Hukmi*).

## 3. Underlying Corporate Rights & Dividends

### 3.1 Dividend Pass-Through
- When underlying corporate shares pay cash dividends, the tokenized asset issuer or custodian credits the equivalent value in $USDC$ to token holders on record date.
- Dividend purification procedures must be observed: if the screened company generates a minor portion of non-operating impermissible income ($< 5\%$), token holders are provided with the exact purification percentage to donate to charity without expectation of reward (*Sadaqah li-tathir al-mal*).

### 3.2 Voting Rights
- Underlying share voting rights are either exercised via issuer proxy policies in accordance with Shariah guidelines or passed through via governance signaling where supported by the issuing custodian.
