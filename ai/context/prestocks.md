# PreStocks & Tokenized Pre-IPO Context

## Overview
PreStocks represents the tokenization of private company equity prior to an Initial Public Offering (e.g., SpaceX, OpenAI, Anthropic, Stripe).

## Mechanics & Legal Structures
- **Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs)**: Private shares are held within an isolated legal entity (e.g., Delaware Series LLC or Cayman Foundation).
- **Tokenized Claims**: Digital tokens issued on Solana represent beneficial ownership of SPV units.
- **Price Discovery Challenges**: Because private company shares trade infrequently on secondary markets, dynamic bonding curves (Meteora DBC) provide the initial continuous liquidity and pricing signal prior to secondary exchange listings.
