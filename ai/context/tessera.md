# Tessera & Fractional Asset Mechanics

## Overview
Tessera pioneered decentralized asset fractionalization protocols, allowing high-value real-world assets (art, real estate, institutional equities) to be held in trust vaults and fractionalized into fungible SPL tokens.

## Application in Equity Catalyst
- Vaults act as non-custodial collective ownership pools.
- Fractional vault share tokens allow retail participants to gain exposure to high-share-price equities (e.g. Berkshire Hathaway Class A, high-ticket private venture rounds) with algorithmic rebalancing and continuous liquidity.
